## Week of August 1, 2026

### Updates

- **More reliable releases.** We rolled out new end-to-end testing infrastructure that lets us seed and tear down realistic data across the app before every release. In practice, this means faster iteration on features you rely on — signups, subscriptions, blog, newsletter, contact forms, and checkout — with fewer regressions along the way.

<Note>
  Nothing about your account, data, or day-to-day workflow changes with this
  update. See the [Quickstart](/quickstart) to get started.
</Note>
