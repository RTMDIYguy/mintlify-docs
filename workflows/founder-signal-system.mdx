The Founder Signal System is the current client-facing starter package. It is a thin vertical slice across six URC Marketing workflows, priced as a starter setup, and intended to move a founder from unclear message to a repeatable weekly or biweekly cycle within a 3-to-5 day setup sprint.

<Note>
  The package is a **Draft** starter (`kit/1.0`, version `0.2.0-draft`). It is not yet Certified. Treat it as the first sellable cut while the underlying source workflows are still being converted.
</Note>

## Who this is for

Use this package with:

- Solo founders or owner-operators of small service-based businesses, roughly 1 to 10 people.
- Founders who already have a few clients and some revenue but publish, reach out, and follow up inconsistently.
- Founders who cannot cleanly answer who they serve, what painful problem they solve, and what to say next.
- Founders who do not want to buy a nine-workflow Marketing department before they have proven the message.

Do not use it with:

- Marketing-mature teams with an existing content engine, paid acquisition stack, or dedicated marketing hire.
- Pre-revenue founders who have no clients to learn from.
- Buyers who want paid ads, a full social calendar, or a website rebuild before running one cycle.

## What it solves

The clearest, most repeated founder pain is the MKT-06 cluster: "what do I say, where do I say it, how often, and is any of it working?"

The Founder Signal System packages the minimum viable dependencies needed to make that content question answerable and the resulting work compoundable. If the founder does not know who they are for, sharpening content is rearranging deck chairs. If the founder writes great content but never follows up, the work leaks at the bottom. The package closes both leaks with a single thin slice.

## The thin-slice flow

The package touches six URC Marketing workflows but only implements the minimum cut from each. MKT-06 is the visible wedge because that is where founders feel the pain first.

| Step | Source workflow | Contribution |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. ICP and fit focus | MKT-01 Lead Generation & Conversion | Target customer, painful problem, desired outcome, urgency, exclusion criteria, qualified-conversation definition. |
| 2. Diagnostic and segmentation | MKT-03 Polls & Assessments | Short founder interview or intake form. No heavy survey at this stage. |
| 3. Content engine | MKT-06 Content Creation & Dissemination | Content brief plus three to five LinkedIn posts, one short email, and one anchor asset outline per cycle. |
| 4. Outreach lane | MKT-05 Outreach & Engagement | Small, named list of 10 to 25 prospects or warm reconnects with human outreach tied to the content signal. |
| 5. Follow-up lane | MKT-02 Email/SMS Nurture | Three-touch outreach and follow-up sequence. Responses segmented into interested, not now, referral, no response. |
| 6. Proof loop | MKT-04 Reviews & Referrals | Captured replies, objections, testimonials, referrals, and traction notes that feed the next cycle. |

Run the cycle weekly or biweekly so the proof loop has time to inform the next round.

## What is intentionally excluded

The package deliberately does not include:

- Paid advertising or PPC strategy (deferred to MKT-07).
- A full social media management calendar (deferred to MKT-08).
- Event or webinar marketing (deferred to MKT-09).
- Heavy automation. The flow assumes the founder uses existing tools and prefers to ship rather than tune.
- A full Microsoft 365, Notion, HubSpot, or Klaviyo build-out.
- Brand identity, visual design, or website rebuilds.
- Sales proposal, contract, or onboarding workflows.

If a buyer needs any of the above, sell this package as the starting point and plan the next layer as a separate engagement.

## Outputs per cycle

- **Signal brief** — one-page ICP plus painful-problem plus founder point of view summary.
- **Content cycle batch** — three to five LinkedIn posts, one short email, and one anchor outline.
- **Outreach list** — 10 to 25 named prospects or warm reconnects.
- **Follow-up segments** — responses split into interested, not now, referral, and no response.
- **Proof log** — captured replies, objections, testimonials, referrals, and traction notes.

## Implementation sprint

Implementers stand the package up over a 3-to-5 day sprint. Budget 60 to 90 minutes per day, then 2 to 4 hours per cycle after setup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Day 1 — ICP and offer focus">
    Fill in the founder intake questions. Produce a one-page signal brief covering ICP, painful problem, desired outcome, exclusion criteria, and qualified-conversation criteria. Stop here if any of it is fuzzy.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 2 — Diagnostic and message map">
    Convert the signal brief into a message map: one-sentence promise, three proof points, one signature founder point of view, one disqualifier. Define a 3-to-5 question diagnostic and decide where it lives (LinkedIn DM, short intro call, or intake form).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 3 — Content brief and first asset batch">
    Fill in one content brief. Draft three to five LinkedIn posts, one short email, and one anchor asset outline. Publish at least one piece today. AI-assist is fine for drafting; the founder personalizes and approves before publishing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 4 — Outreach and follow-up setup">
    Build a 10-to-25 person outreach list. Write personal first-touch messages. Set up the three-touch outreach and follow-up sequence so the second and third touches are scheduled before the first goes out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Day 5 — Proof capture and next-cycle decision">
    Log every reply, objection, testimonial, referral, and traction note in the proof capture template. Review the log and decide what changes for the next cycle: message, channel, audience, or offer.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Automation paths

The manual Markdown package is the default and requires no third-party connector. Two optional automation paths are provided for implementers who want to accelerate the intake-to-brief step.

### n8n v1 runtime (preferred)

As of 2026-05-17, n8n is the preferred v1 runtime for this package. The runtime handoff clones or adapts the tested MKT-06 pattern into the Founder Signal intake flow. Use this path when local n8n is available and the buyer wants a working automation on day one.

### Make.com Standard-tier template (deferred)

A draft Make.com blueprint and a public-template publisher checklist are staged in the package. The Standard-tier Magic Template Link is not live yet — the Make.com free plan blocked activation even after manual scenario build. To activate the link:

1. Publish the scenario from Make.com.
2. Generate the public sharing link.
3. Test the link from a second account or team.
4. Replace the placeholder in the package's `template.md`.

The Standard-tier path requires Make.com plus OpenAI. HubSpot and Microsoft 365 modules are removable buyer-side conveniences.

## When to use manual vs. automation

- **Manual Markdown package** — buyer does not want to connect Make.com, OpenAI, HubSpot, or Microsoft 365, or wants to prove the message before touching automation. This is the fallback in all cases.
- **n8n v1 runtime** — implementer already runs local n8n and wants the intake-to-brief step automated on day one.
- **Make.com Standard-tier link** — deferred. Do not promise this path to buyers until the public sharing link is live and tested.

## Verification

The package is considered to have completed one cycle when all of the following are true:

- Intake questions are filled out.
- One content brief is filled in and at least one piece is published.
- An outreach list of 10 or more names is contacted with at least one human touch.
- One three-touch outreach and follow-up sequence has fired.
- The proof capture log has at least one logged reply, objection, testimonial, referral, or traction note.

The Standard-tier Magic Template Link is not considered live until the real Make.com public sharing link is in `template.md` and the install has been tested from a second account or team.

## Common failure modes

| Failure | Mitigation |
| --- | --- |
| Founder cannot describe who this is and is not for in two sentences. | Hold publishing until the intake is complete and a one-page signal brief is signed off. |
| Founder publishes but never runs outreach. | The checklist forces outreach setup on Day 4, in the same week as the first content batch. |
| First outreach goes out, but the second or third touch never follows. | The three-touch template is created on Day 4 alongside the outreach list. |
| Replies and testimonials arrive but never get logged. | Proof capture runs every cycle and is the explicit Day 5 step. |
| Buyer asks for paid ads, full social calendar, or website rebuild before the thin slice has produced proof. | Point back to the "What is intentionally excluded" list and scope the next layer as a separate engagement. |
